Our approach

Good housing is about more than a room.

Texas Authentic Living offers a respectful, home-like setting for adults navigating change. We focus on comfortable accommodations, practical household essentials, and clear communication with residents and referral partners.

  • Independent and transitional housing
  • Furnished shared and private bedroom options
  • Access to shared household spaces
  • Coordination with referring organizations
  • Veteran-owned and managed

Residents must be able to live independently. We do not provide medical treatment, nursing, medication administration, personal care, or included meal service. Housing is subject to suitability and availability.

Who we serve

Support for life's transitions.

Our housing may be suitable for independent adults seeking a stable living arrangement following a change in circumstances, including Veterans, individuals transitioning from housing instability, and eligible adults after hospital or rehabilitation discharge.
